The Problem

Tracking money across Google Sheets, bank apps, and memory doesn't work. Subscriptions slip through. Bills surprise you. Debts feel endless. You need one place that shows you the full picture — not a bloated app, not a spreadsheet, not a pinjol.

📺

📋

Architecture

Database

12 tables. SQLite. All amounts in INTEGER (Rupiah). No floating point money.

TablePurpose
categoriesIncome, budget, subscription, bill, debt categories with colors
income_entriesMonthly income sources
budget_allocationsHow much allocated per category per month
subscriptionsActive/paused/cancelled subscriptions with billing cycle
subscription_paymentsPayment log for subscriptions
recurring_billsFixed recurring bills (electricity, internet, etc.)
bill_paymentsPer-month bill payment status
debtsActive debts with principal, interest, payoff tracking
debt_paymentsPayment log for debts
ai_conversationsChat conversation history
ai_messagesIndividual messages with tool call audit trail
settingsKey-value preferences

AI System

Vercel AI SDK connects to Deepseek API. The AI has 8 function-calling tools that can read and write to the database. Responses stream progressively. All tool calls are logged for transparency.

Deployment

Docker container on homelab Ubuntu VM. App listens on 127.0.0.1:3000. Cloudflare Tunnel provides public access with SSL. SQLite file mounted as volume. Daily backup via cron.

What Stasis Is Not

Clear boundaries keep the product focused and the scope manageable.

❌ No Bank Sync

Manual entry only. Indonesian bank APIs are unreliable. Your data stays local.

❌ No One-Off Expenses

No coffee, no snacks, no impulse buys. Stasis tracks recurring commitments only.

❌ No Multi-User

Single user. Personal. No family plans, no sharing, no social.

❌ No Investments

No stocks, crypto, or portfolio tracking. This is for subscriptions, bills, debts, and budgets.
